# Confirmed four/six-camera monitoring

This implementation integrates FR013/FR015/FR018/FR020/FR067 with the capture interface, pose worker, product model jobs, saved review and cases. It is an experimental local monitoring feature. Synthetic automated checks are not pharmacy detection-accuracy or physical-site acceptance; **0/6 pharmacy installations have been observed or commissioned by these tests**.

## Operator flow

Connect the existing CCTV screen, camera or recording. Inspect the proposed or manually chosen 2×2, 3×2 or 2×3 layout and press **Use this layout**. Choose **All 4/6 confirmed cameras**. Enable all-camera product analysis and choose automatic rotation or manual **Analyse next ready camera**, then explicitly start detection. Selected-camera mode remains available and keeps its existing ten-second product cadence.

The confirmed source-normalized boxes are used unchanged for both pose and product crops. Tiles in the layout preview indicate the active camera set; the buttons in the per-camera product cards select which sample strip staff inspect. Selecting a card changes inspection only. It does not switch, pause or prioritize the processing schedule. A source, layout, border, custom-area, dimensions, branch or mode change stops the old run and disarms its sound. Reconnect/reconfirm when required and explicitly start/enable again. Detection remains visible-page/awake-laptop only.

## Bounded local processing

One worker loads MediaPipe in **IMAGE** mode for the all-camera run. It receives at most one cropped bitmap at once. Each camera has a separate `LiveBehaviourEngine`, so Person #1 in Camera 1 never transfers temporal history to Person #1 in Camera 2. IMAGE mode prevents MediaPipe VIDEO tracking state from mixing camera pictures. The worker is configured for at most four poses per camera frame, so crowded-camera coverage beyond that bound is not provided. The existing 1.2-second movement continuity limit remains unchanged. Sampling is targeted every 500 ms per camera; a minimum 50-ms inter-work delay and a delay equal to completed inference time bound duty. Slow laptops visibly lose per-camera continuity instead of inventing movement between distant observations. A one-second worker ticket deadline closes the worker and stops monitoring; no bitmap backlog exists. Body-processing cards show actual completed/target opportunities, misses and last-frame age.

Each camera independently observes source continuity every 100 ms and samples approximately every 1.25 seconds into its own four-frame buffer. At most two JPEG encodes are attempted on a heartbeat, staggering work across cameras. Product crops retain their actual detail, with maximum 768-pixel edge and no upscaling. The submitted four images and times remain frozen while other cameras collect newer samples.

The product model has **one global active job**, no queued job backlog, at least two seconds between global starts and a ten-second per-camera target. Ready cameras rotate fairly. Six cameras therefore require at least 12 seconds for repeated complete rounds even with instant inference; a slower model takes longer. The ten-second target is deliberately reported as a target, with measured start interval and missed target opportunities shown per camera. Missed sampling opportunities are not missing-person or theft-recall measurements. Camera buffers do not imply inference completion. The backend retains its installation-wide slot guard when another tab or a cancelled provider call still holds capacity.

## Evidence, review and sound

Each product/live observation declares a capture-instance UUID, epoch, layout, zero-based camera index, original source dimensions and normalized crop. The API validates geometry and JPEG dimensions and derives a camera label. These are reported screen positions, not proof of physical camera identity or authentic footage. Source and geometry remain visible in historical review and linked cases. Case creation retains existing staff review, branch authority, stale-state/idempotency and evidence-expiry rules; linking does not extend retention.

All-camera movement-rule sound is disabled. Product attention sound defaults off. In a running camera set, staff explicitly request a bounded sound test, confirm they heard it and then arm. Recorded tests require a separately labelled consent checkbox. A fresh possible-concealment observation can request one eight-second tone; a global thirty-second cooldown and per-job duplicate protection apply across cameras. The visual panel identifies the camera and requires review, never a theft conclusion. Acknowledgement, mute/volume change, stop, hidden page, source/context loss or runtime interruption can stop/disarm audio. Recovery and historical refresh never rearm or replay past observations. Software cannot establish physical speaker audibility; the staff confirmation and on-site test remain required.

## Verification boundaries

`multiCameraInteractions.test.ts` covers independent buffers, four/six fairness and start bounds, frozen submitted sequences, cancelled capacity, malformed camera sets, continuity changes, partial capture failure, measured cadence and missed opportunities. `all-camera.spec.ts` runs real isolated pilot APIs, SQLite and encrypted evidence with synthetic canvas camera grids. Only the vision/provider and pose-result boundaries are faked for deterministic scenarios; JPEG encoding/cropping, real API geometry validation, encrypted saved images, authentication, review and case creation are exercised. Additional cases cover layout/source/resolution/mode/branch/offline cancellation, explicit sound commissioning/cooldown, slow/hung workers and phone accessibility. The coordinating release report records exact passing commands and any remaining failures.

Real pharmacy angles, lighting, crowding, pharmacy-device performance, CCTV compatibility, model accuracy/false alarms, sleep/resume and physical speakers still require per-branch acceptance. This slice does not provide background/hidden-page capture, simultaneous six-camera inference, face recognition, cross-camera identity, POS reconciliation, or guaranteed theft detection.
