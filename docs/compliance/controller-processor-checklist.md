# Controller and processor checklist

Complete before uploading any pharmacy evidence.

- [ ] Pharmacy legal entity and controller contact recorded.
- [ ] AisleSignals operating legal entity and processor contact recorded.
- [ ] Article 28 processing terms signed and versioned.
- [ ] Subject matter, duration, nature, purposes, data and people documented.
- [ ] Documented-instruction and confidentiality duties recorded.
- [ ] Security measures and incident-notification route recorded.
- [ ] Subprocessors, locations and objection/change process recorded.
- [ ] International-transfer position and safeguards recorded.
- [ ] Rights-request and DPIA assistance responsibilities recorded.
- [ ] Deletion/return and backup-expiry terms recorded.
- [ ] Audit/information rights and termination assistance recorded.
- [ ] Support access is scoped, time-limited and audited.
- [ ] Camera credentials remain local and are excluded from support data.
- [ ] No patient, prescription, payment or audio data is authorised.

Attach the executed agreement reference to the private branch compliance record;
do not commit customer agreements to this repository.
