# Legitimate-interests assessment template

Use this only when the pharmacy proposes legitimate interests as its Article 6
basis. The pharmacy controller must complete and approve it.

## Purpose test

- State the concrete security purpose and benefit.
- Identify whose legitimate interests are pursued.
- Confirm the purpose is lawful, specific and current.
- Separate loss prevention from staff performance management, marketing and
  unrelated analytics.

## Necessity test

- Explain how AisleSignals contributes to the purpose.
- List less intrusive options tried or considered.
- Explain why each enabled camera, zone, observation and evidence type is
  necessary.
- Record why metadata-only operation is or is not sufficient.

## Balancing test

Consider customers, children, employees, patients and passers-by. Document
reasonable expectations, location, visibility of notices, data sensitivity,
scale, false-positive consequences, power imbalance, safeguards, opt-out or
objection handling, and the likely impact of misuse or disclosure.
