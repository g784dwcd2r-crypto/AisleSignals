# Staff privacy and evidence procedure

## Start of shift

1. Sign in with the assigned account and MFA; never share credentials.
2. Confirm the displayed branch, assigned reviewer and camera-health state.
3. Report an incorrect view, missing privacy mask, frozen stream or unexpected
   screen immediately and suspend that view.
4. Confirm required entrance signs and the full notice remain available.

## Reviewing an alert

1. Treat the alert as an observation requiring review.
2. Check source freshness, camera coverage, confidence facts and uncertainty.
3. Review only the minimum necessary evidence.
4. Record observable facts and choose benign, insufficient evidence, suspected
   incident or manager-confirmed loss according to branch policy.
5. Never label a person as a thief, announce an accusation, or use an AI result
   as the sole basis for intervention.
6. Use established pharmacy safety and emergency procedures; AisleSignals does
   not replace them.

## Evidence handling

- Do not photograph the screen, download locally, message, email or share footage
  outside the authorised export flow.
- Do not search another branch or use earlier events to infer identity.
- Record the purpose before playback, export, disclosure or a legal hold.
- Report misdirected access, lost devices, suspicious login, improper disclosure
  or a missing deletion immediately through **[incident route]**.

## Manager duties

- Review access and pending invitations regularly.
- Remove departing staff and revoke lost devices immediately.
- Review holds, retention failures, overdue requests and support grants.
- Suspend evidence mode after material camera, purpose, model, recipient,
  retention or hosting changes until compliance approval is renewed.
