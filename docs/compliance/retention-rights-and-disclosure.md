## Rules

- Expiry is automatic and testable; access must fail when deletion is pending or
  integrity cannot be confirmed.
- A legal hold has a documented reason, scope, owner, start, review date and end
  condition. It cannot extend unrelated material.
- Restore procedures reapply revocations, deletions and expired retention.
- Offboarding immediately revokes people/devices, then follows the approved
  export, deletion and backup-expiry schedule.
- No material is retained indefinitely "just in case".

## Rights requests

Record receipt, identity-verification method, scope, deadline, search sources,
third-party redaction, exemptions relied on, decision, disclosure channel and
completion. Restrict searches to the relevant branch and time range. Do not
disclose unredacted images of unrelated people merely because one requester is
visible.

## External disclosure

Require an authorised manager, documented purpose, recipient identity, legal
authority or request reference, selected evidence variants, integrity manifest,
expiry and audit entry. A detector score is context, not proof of an offence.
The product must never send evidence automatically to police, insurers, social
media or another pharmacy.
