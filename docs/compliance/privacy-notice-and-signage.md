# Privacy notice and signage template

Replace every bracketed field and have the controller approve the result.

## Entrance sign: first layer

> CCTV and automated video analysis operate in this pharmacy for
> **[specific security purpose]**. The system flags observable interactions for
> trained staff review; it does not independently determine theft. Controller:
> **[legal entity]**. Contact: **[privacy contact]**. Full information, retention
> and rights: **[short URL and accessible in-store route]**.

Place the sign before a person enters the monitored area. Check visibility,
lighting, readable type and an accessible alternative during commissioning.

## Full notice: second layer

Include:

- controller identity, address and privacy/DPO contact;
- each specific purpose and lawful basis selected by the controller;
- a plain explanation of local automated analysis and mandatory human review;
- categories of data, cameras/areas and whether snapshots or clips are created;
- recipients and processor/subprocessor categories;
- hosting locations and transfer safeguards where relevant;
- separately justified retention periods for recordings, dismissed alerts,
  incidents, exports, audit data and backups;
- access, objection, restriction, erasure and complaint rights, with the route
  for submitting a request;
- the right to complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission;
- any consequence of not providing data, if applicable;
- the current notice version, effective date and material-change history.

Do not claim that all recorded people will receive an individual notice. Do not
describe a detector output as proof, a conviction or a theft determination.
